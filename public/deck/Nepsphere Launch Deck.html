Why we built this

The Facebook groups
did so much.
We can do more.

For years, three groups carried us — apartment hunts, ride shares, Dashain plates, F1 advice, lost passports found in 20 minutes.

But threads scroll away. Posts get buried. Scammers slip in. The same questions get asked every Tuesday.

We deserve a real home.
Warm. Organized. Ours.

The promise

Three things we'll always be.

01

Local-first.

Dallas before USA. USA before global. Your chapter is the front door — not an afterthought tucked under a filter.

02

Real people only.

Every member is verified with government ID + selfie. The orange check means someone we trust stood behind that profile.

03

Free, with respect.

No ads. No selling your data. The basics — posting, joining rooms, verification — stay free forever.

Product · Connect

Three intents.
Never confused.

Pick what you're here for. We'll only show you people looking for the same thing.

Friendship

Make a chai friend.

For new arrivals, lonely weekends, or anyone wanting platonic Nepali friends nearby. No pressure, no pretending.

Dating

Meet someone real.

Verified profiles, clear intentions, no ghosts. For grown-ups who'd like dinner with someone who gets the family WhatsApp.

Marriage

For something serious.

Family-aware, intention-clear. Background sections for caste, dietary, language preferences — only if you choose to share.

⚠

No one accidentally swiped on an aunty looking for a chai friend. Intent isolation is a feature.

Three small things.

2

The rest comes naturally — that's how community works.
